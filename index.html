Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free and come with no pressure. Call or send a request and we'll get back to you promptly.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Most homes benefit from a cleaning at least twice a year — typically late fall and spring. Homes near many trees may need it more often.

Why does dryer vent cleaning matter?

Lint trapped in the vent makes your dryer work harder, lengthens drying times and is a common cause of house fires. A regular cleaning keeps it efficient and safer.

How often should a chimney be cleaned?

If you use your fireplace regularly, an annual cleaning and safety check is a good rule of thumb. We can also repair damage we find along the way.

Can you handle several jobs in one visit?

Absolutely. That's the benefit of one team for six services — for example, gutters, painting touch-ups and small repairs in a single visit.
