Good to know

Why chimney care matters

  • Reduces creosote build-up, a common cause of chimney fires
  • Helps smoke vent properly instead of backing into the room
  • Catches cracks and damage early, before they get costly
  • Keeps your fireplace ready for the cooler months

How often?

If you use your fireplace regularly, an annual cleaning and safety check is a good rule of thumb. We can also repair damage we find along the way.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a chimney be cleaned?

If you use your fireplace regularly, an annual cleaning and safety check is a good rule of thumb. We can also repair damage we find along the way.

What happens during a chimney cleaning?

We protect your floor, sweep the flue to remove soot and build-up, and check what we can reach for damage or blockages. We'll tell you about anything that needs repair.
